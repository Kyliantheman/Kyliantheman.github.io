Who Am I?

I'm Kylian, a beginning ICT-student at the HZ university of applied sciences. I enjoy spending time with friends, family and my lovely girlfriend Angélica. In my free time, I like to play video games and workout, I also play waterpolo where team building is important.

I have a passion for technology and I'm always eager to learn new things. My goal is to become a skilled professional in the ICT field and contribute to innovative projects that make a difference in the world.

Daily life

Waterpolo

I've been playing waterpolo from a young age. From the age of 6 all the way to the big age of 20 (as of now) I've had the luxury of having an amazing time whilst also improving myself. Playing waterpolo empties your head after a long hard day at work or school, it activates your muscles and you forget about all the hardships in daily life. Waterpolo is not only about improving yourself, it's also a teams sport, you get to have amazing team mates who I enjoy spending time with not only at matches and training, but also outside of the swimming pool. Making friends, building connection and also having the best time of your life makes waterpolo one of the best experiences of my life!

Friends

Friends are very important in my life. They not only support me in difficult times, but they also make life much more fun and brighter. They definitely have a special place in my heart. Friends prevent you from isolating yourself, they make you do things you normally wouldn't, they motivate you to push yourself, to give it your all. Traveling with friends, partying with friends, gaming with friends, excercising with friends, being stupid with friends all contribute to your lifes story!

Girlfriend

My girlfriend is the most important person in my life. Traveling, shopping, and just spending time with her makes me the happiest person on this planet. She helps me with problems I can't solve on my own, she carries me through hardships I would have a difficult time with. She supports my descicions and helps me make the right call, I also like that she critisizes me when i'm being stupid. She motivates me to move forward, to help me pick a direction which positively influences me. ---
